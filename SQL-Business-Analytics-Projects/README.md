# SQL Business Analytics Projects
This portfolio demonstrates the practical application of SQL for business analytics, database management, data preparation, and analytical reporting.
The projects showcase my ability to structure business data, perform CRUD operations, apply business rules, manage employee and organizational datasets, and prepare structured data for further analysis and visualization.

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- MySQL
- Database Creation
- Table Creation and Schema Design
- CRUD Operations
- INSERT, SELECT, UPDATE and DELETE
- Conditional Data Updates
- Data Filtering
- Employee and Workforce Data Management
- Schema Modification
- Business Data Preparation
- Data Management for Analytics and Reporting

## Business Application
The SQL projects demonstrate how structured organizational data can be managed and prepared for business analysis. The datasets include employee information such as department, location, salary, joining date and age, which can support workforce reporting and management analysis.

These projects form part of my broader Business Analytics and Data Analytics portfolio, demonstrating the data-management foundation required for dashboard development, business insights, and stakeholder reporting.
